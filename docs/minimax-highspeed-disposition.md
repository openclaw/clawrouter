# Frozen MiniMax M2.7 highspeed disposition

The E01 row `minimax/MiniMax-M2.7-highspeed` preserves that exact upstream
identity on the existing MiniMax OpenAI-compatible Chat route. The existing
M2.7 default, credentials, endpoint and price card are unchanged.

Primary evidence checked 2026-11-04:

- The [release record](https://platform.minimax.io/docs/release-notes/models)
  names the M2.7 and M2.7-highspeed series introduced on 2026-03-18.
- The dated [model notice](https://platform.minimax.io/docs/faq/models) excludes
  new users from the M2.7 family from 2026-10-19. Existing PAYG users may continue
  until retirement on 2026-11-18; after that, PAYG calls will no longer work.
- The same notice describes a separate M Plan/Token Plan automatic migration to
  M3.1 Flash from 2026-10-19. That subscription behavior does not authorize
  rewriting this PAYG identity or inheriting M3.1 prices, facts or access.
- [Current pricing](https://platform.minimax.io/docs/pricing/overview) does not
  provide an M2.7-highspeed rate. Search caches and other providers' prices do
  not qualify a current MiniMax PAYG tariff.

The dated schedule controls the November 4 disposition even though the model
list groups the family under a retired heading. `status: deprecated` records
the already-effective restriction without declaring every existing account
stopped early. The future retirement is documented, not encoded as a fake
current stop or an invented runtime timer. A later dated qualification must
mark the PAYG identity stopped once the published retirement takes effect.

There is no price, tier, output/context limit or native modality fact on this
row. Unknown costs fail closed under caller and provider hard-dollar budgets;
explicit policy-fixed charges remain separately labeled. An eligible route is
not account entitlement, evidence of live availability or authorization to use
a subscription credential. No paid probe, live login or account change is made.
