# Fusion on fresh URI repair tasks

The cascade, independent strong-only and independent cheap-only each passed
**4/4** historical URI repair tasks. The cascade cost 66.60% less and took
8.04% less aggregate workflow time than strong-only. It still **failed the
fusion-value gate**: cheap-only matched its quality, cost 80.39% less than the
cascade and took 25.59% less time.

These are four new campaign tasks, selected by a frozen six-candidate
qualification before any model calls. They come from one public repository;
local freshness does not establish absence from model training data.
[Sanitized records](fusion-uri-transfer-study.json) retain all six
qualifications, twelve arms and thirteen calls.

## Results

| Strategy | Full success | Estimated model cost | Workflow time |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Independent cheap-only | 4/4 | $0.012400 | 55.53 s |
| Independent strong-only | 4/4 | $0.189369 | 81.15 s |
| Cheap-first, one strong repair | 4/4 | $0.063243 | 74.62 s |

The full run cost $0.265012. Cache-neutral strong and cascade costs were
$0.155844 and $0.058632, respectively: a 62.38% reduction. Cache-neutral cost
removes caching effects, but retains actual input/output token counts; it is
not necessarily greater than estimated cost. Verification infrastructure is
unpriced.

One valid anchored cascade draft failed public tests and received a successful
strong repair. Independent cheap-only passed that same task on its own draw.
There were no false accepts or lost strong successes. The repair demonstrates
recovery within an arm, not a quality advantage over the independent control.

The frozen gate requires quality at least equal to strong-only and strictly
above cheap-only, zero false accepts or lost strong successes, at least 20%
estimated and cache-neutral cost savings, and no aggregate slowdown versus
strong-only. The cost and latency checks passed; the cheap-quality check failed.

## Qualification and controls

The six-candidate roster was fixed before sandbox results or inference. Each
candidate uses its original broken parent source and the upstream repair as a
reference. Original changed tests plus parse, equality, resolution and
serialization suites are retained where present. One unchanged test group per
eligible file is withheld from model-visible tests. Only `index.js` is editable;
base production library files remain available. AJV and uri-js compatibility
suites are excluded because their dependencies are not in the cached harness.

Every reference passed both suites. Admitted broken parents failed both suites
with exactly the reference assertion counts. The two excluded parents aborted
before completing their assertion plans; they received no model calls and were
not replaced. No tests or snapshots were generated by models.

GPT-6 Luna supplied cheap attempts and GPT-6.1 Terra supplied strong attempts,
with medium reasoning and an 8,192-token output limit. Independent arms use
separate calls and a frozen hash-seeded order. A rejected cascade draft can
receive one strong repair using only its own proposal and public failure.
Full grading starts after all live arms finish.

All arms enable the same opt-in unique exact-source anchor recovery used in the
[preceding development study](fusion-anchor-recovery-study.md). Ambiguous
matches stay rejected. The larger source context required an explicit 300 KB
request bound for this experiment; the prior 250 KB default remains unchanged.
The first preflight rejected the undersized bound before any calls or charges.

The pinned TAP16/Tape5.7.5 harness, runtime image and existing compute were reused.
This is not the upstream package-manager environment. Workflow time includes
admission, request preparation, durable accounting, decoding, public testing
and cleanup; subsequent full grading and replay are excluded. A model-free
replay matched all outcomes and costs and retained original measured times.

## Decision

Do not promote this result as a fusion gain or change routing defaults. The
independent small model is the better measured choice for these four tasks.
This extends the evidence beyond the previously exposed LRU tasks, but one
small historical sample and one draw per arm cannot establish a production
policy or the causal effect of anchor recovery. Private source and replay
artifacts are retained; the sanitized records are not a turnkey reproduction.

No deployment or runtime-default change accompanies this report.
